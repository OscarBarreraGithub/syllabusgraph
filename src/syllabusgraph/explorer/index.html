HOW IT WORKS

Bring the material. Keep the result.

01

Set up your workspace

Your agent installs the tool and creates a private materials folder. Drop files there or point the agent to them. Agree on scope and models before extraction starts.

02

Build and review

Extract concepts and relationships with source references. An independent critic checks the work. Save checkpoints so you can continue another day.

03

Explore and reuse

Keep a graph for each source and compare them in a shared graph. Later, choose teaching goals and build a course. Share your derived graphs while keeping source books private.
