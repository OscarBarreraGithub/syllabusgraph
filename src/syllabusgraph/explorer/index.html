A REUSABLE WORKFLOW

Your material.
Your course.

The graph describes the subject. The course describes a journey through it. Keep them separate, and the same knowledge can support many ways of teaching.

01 / GATHER

Bring your sources.

Drop references into your private materials folder, or give your agent a file path. Together, set the scope.

02 / CONNECT

Build the graph.

An extractor proposes concepts and dependencies. A separate critic checks them against the source. Decisions keep their evidence.

03 / SHAPE

Design a course.

Then choose goals, student background, and available time. Explore a sequence, adjust it, and export a teaching plan.

04 / SHARE

Pass it on.

Share reviewed graphs and course plans. Keep textbooks, working extracts, and private guidance on your computer.

Creating a graph uses your model allowance.
