FROM SOURCES TO A COURSE

A graph you can read.

Start with the concepts shared across books and the connections between them. Open a concept to inspect its source evidence. The shared graph connects treatments across books; each textbook graph keeps its own account.

  1. Bring your material. Your agent makes a private materials folder and helps establish scope.
  2. Build reviewed knowledge. An extractor proposes concepts and dependencies. A separate critic checks the evidence.
  3. Shape a course later. Choose goals, entry knowledge, and time once the graph is ready.
